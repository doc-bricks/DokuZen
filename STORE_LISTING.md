## English

**Short title (≤ 30 chars)**
DokuZen

**Description (≤ 10,000 chars)**
DokuZen is a local document workstation for the Windows desktop.
All processing runs exclusively on your device — no cloud, no accounts, no
data transmission.

**Core features:**

- **PDF editing:** Add, remove, reorder, merge and split pages.
- **OCR:** Text recognition in scanned PDFs and images (Tesseract engine,
  offline operation).
- **Redaction:** Permanently obscure confidential text areas.
- **Conversion:** PDF ↔ Word/DOCX, images to searchable PDFs.
- **File library:** Manage and preview your document collection with full-text
  search.
- **Batch processing:** Process multiple documents simultaneously.

**Privacy:** No network connections, no telemetry, no hidden background processes.
Full AGPL-3.0-or-later open-source code available for inspection.

**System requirements:** Windows 10/11 (64-bit)

Local PDF workstation: edit, OCR, redact, convert. No cloud, no accounts —
everything on your device.
